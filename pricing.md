## How pricing works

- **Quantity** is the biggest factor: the price per unit drops as the order grows.
- **Size and material** change how much board is used (paperboard, cardstock, corrugated, rigid greyboard, kraft).
- **Print and finishes** add cost per unit: full colour inside and out, lamination, foil, spot UV, embossing.
- **Inserts and windows** add cost per unit.
- **Included free:** dieline, digital proof, Australia-wide shipping. Physical samples are free; you pay sample shipping.
